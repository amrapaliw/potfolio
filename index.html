I'm a financial analyst who bridges the gap between raw data and executive decisions. My work spans FP&A modeling, corporate finance, and domain-specific healthcare finance — building tools that don't just report numbers but explain what they mean and what to do next.

At Humana, I work on Medicare Advantage and commercial plan finance — analyzing medical cost trends, building rolling forecast models, and protecting at-risk revenue through MLR and member retention analysis. I hold a Master's in Management of Technology from Arizona State University (2025) and a second Master's in Computer Science & Engineering — which means I can work across the quant and business stack.
